abstract: Most state-of-the-art large language models (LLMs) are trained mainly on English data, limiting their effectiveness on non-English, especially low-resource, languages. This study investigates whether language adapters can facilitate cross-lingual transfer in English-centric LLMs. We train language adapters for 13 languages using Llama 2 (7B) and Llama 3.1 (8B) as base models, and evaluate their effectiveness on two downstream tasks (MLQA and SIB-200) using either task adapters or in-context learning. Our results reveal that language adapters improve performance for languages not seen during pre-training, but provide negligible benefit for seen languages. These findings highlight the limitations of language adapters as a general solution for multilingual adaptation in English-centric LLMs.
